How it works

Three steps from idea to armed position

01

Build & review

Pick strikes off the live chain, choose a structure, and check payoff and Greeks before you commit to anything.

02

Deploy through your broker

Send the strategy to your connected broker account. Orders execute exactly where your capital already sits.

03

Arm exit rules

Set stop-loss, target and trailing-SL. The rule sits on ApnaAlgo's server, watching the position after you're gone.

A server-side rule can only reduce or exit a position that already exists. It can never open a new live position on its own — armed rules watch and close, they don't initiate.
